Changelog(Web)

2025-08-05

New

Holiday Calendars: You can now subscribe to holiday calendars for different countries and regions. Once subscribed, holidays will appear in the Sidebar, Today list, and Calendar view for easier planning. Go to Settings > Integrations & Import > Holidays to add.
